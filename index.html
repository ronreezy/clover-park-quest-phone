Clover Park Quest

Welcome to Clover Park Technical College

Help Alice learn about the programs at Clover Park Technical College.

Turn your phone sideways to play.

Play with touch or a keyboard.

Walk and talkHold the pad, arrow keys or WASD to walk. Tap Talk or press Enter to talk and continue. Tap a choice, or use the arrows and Enter.
Map and binderOpen Menu or use M for the campus map and B for the card binder. The Entry Coordinator gives Alice her map.
Save your questUse Menu or press K to save and L to load. Return to this page in the same browser. Wait a few seconds after saving.

A student project set on the CPTC Lakewood campus.
